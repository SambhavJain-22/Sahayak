What is Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY: Ayushman Bharat Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY) is a health insurance scheme of the Government of India. It was launched on 23 September 2018. It gives cashless treatment in hospitals for serious illnesses to eligible families.

Coverage amount under Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY: Eligible families get health cover of up to Rs 5 lakh per family per year for secondary and tertiary care hospitalisation. The cover is on a family floater basis, so the amount can be used by any member of the family.

Family size and age under Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY: There is no cap on family size or age of members in the scheme, so all members of an eligible family can be covered.

Cashless and paperless treatment under Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY: Treatment is cashless and paperless at empanelled public and private hospitals. The beneficiary does not need to pay at the hospital for covered treatments.

Pre-existing diseases under Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY: Pre-existing medical conditions are covered from day one of enrolment, with no waiting period.

Pre and post hospitalisation under Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY: The scheme also covers expenses before admission and after discharge, including diagnostics and medicines, for a limited number of days as per scheme rules.

Eligibility for Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY: Eligibility is based on the deprivation and occupational criteria of the Socio-Economic Caste Census (SECC) 2011 for rural and urban families. There is no enrolment fee. Eligible families are identified from this database, so people cannot simply apply on their own without being in the list.

Senior citizens under Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY: Citizens aged 70 years and above are eligible for the scheme irrespective of their income, under the Ayushman Vay Vandana card.

How to check eligibility for Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY: You can check whether your family is eligible on the official website pmjay.gov.in using the "Am I Eligible" option with your mobile number or ration card details, or by calling the helpline, or by visiting an Ayushman Mitra at an empanelled hospital or a Common Service Centre (CSC).

How to get the Ayushman card under Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY: Eligible beneficiaries can get their Ayushman card made at a Common Service Centre (CSC), at an empanelled hospital's Ayushman Mitra desk, or through the official Ayushman app. Keep Aadhaar and your ration card or another valid ID ready.

Finding hospitals under Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY: Treatment is available at empanelled hospitals across India. You can find the list of empanelled hospitals on pmjay.gov.in under the "Find Hospital" option. At each empanelled hospital an Ayushman Mitra helps beneficiaries.

Portability under Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY: The scheme is portable across India. A beneficiary can take treatment at any empanelled hospital in any state, not only in their home state.

What is not covered under Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY: The scheme covers hospitalisation. Outpatient (OPD) consultations and routine medicines without hospital admission are generally not covered.

Helpline for Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY: You can call the official Ayushman Bharat helpline at 14555 or 1800-111-565 for any questions or complaints. The official website is pmjay.gov.in.
